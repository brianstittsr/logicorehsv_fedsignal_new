# FedSignal Marketing Plan
## How to Reach $500K ARR and 150 Customers in Year 1

**Date:** May 12, 2026  
**Target:** 50 HBCUs + 100 Minority-Owned Businesses = $500K ARR  
**Average Revenue Per Customer:** ~$3,333/year

---

## Executive Summary

To reach Year 1 targets, FedSignal needs to acquire **12-15 new customers per month** with a ** blended CAC of $500-800**. The strategy focuses on three core pillars:

1. **HBCU Network Activation** (40% of acquisition) - Leverage existing relationships and conferences
2. **Digital Product-Led Growth** (35% of acquisition) - Self-service funnel with content marketing
3. **Partnership Channel** (25% of acquisition) - SBA, MBDA, HBCU consortia partnerships

**Total Marketing Budget Year 1:** $120,000-150,000 (24-30% of target ARR)

---

## Year 1 Customer Acquisition Strategy

### Target Breakdown

#### HBCU Segment (50 customers, ~$170K ARR)
- **Target List:** 107 accredited HBCUs in the US
- **Segment A (Top 25):** Research-focused HBCUs with existing federal contracts
  - Examples: Howard, Spelman, Morehouse, Hampton, Tuskegee, Florida A&M, NC A&T
  - **Goal:** 15 customers (60% conversion of top 25)
  - **Strategy:** Direct outreach, personalized demos, president/chancellor level engagement
  
- **Segment B (Next 40):** Mid-tier HBCUs with growing research programs
  - **Goal:** 25 customers (62% conversion)
  - **Strategy:** Conference presence, consortium partnerships, webinar series
  
- **Segment C (Remaining 42):** Smaller HBCUs with limited federal contracting
  - **Goal:** 10 customers (24% conversion)
  - **Strategy:** Freemium model, grant-funded onboarding, group training

#### Minority-Owned Business Segment (100 customers, ~$330K ARR)
- **Target List:** ~10,000 certified minority-owned federal contractors
- **Segment A (High-Growth 8(a)):** $5-15M revenue, active federal contractors
  - **Goal:** 30 customers
  - **Strategy:** LinkedIn outreach, case study marketing, referral program
  
- **Segment B (Emerging Small Business):** <$5M revenue, new to federal contracting
  - **Goal:** 50 customers
  - **Strategy:** Content marketing, SEO, SBA workshop partnerships, free tools
  
- **Segment C (HBCU Spin-offs):** Companies founded by HBCU alumni/faculty
  - **Goal:** 20 customers
  - **Strategy:** HBCU alumni networks, entrepreneurship centers

### Channel 1: HBCU Direct Sales ($45,000)

#### Strategy
Direct, high-touch engagement with HBCU leadership and research offices to establish FedSignal as the go-to platform for HBCU government contracting.

#### Tactics

**1. HBCU President/Chancellor Outreach Program ($15,000)**
- **Target:** Top 50 HBCUs
- **Approach:** Personalized letters from FedSignal CEO/Founder
- **Offer:** Free 6-month pilot + dedicated onboarding specialist
- **Materials:** Custom proposal highlighting HBCU-specific benefits
- **Follow-up:** Executive briefings, campus visits
- **Timeline:** Months 1-3
- **Expected:** 20 meetings, 10 conversions

**2. HBCU Research Office Direct Sales ($20,000)**
- **Target:** Research directors, sponsored programs offices
- **Approach:** LinkedIn outreach, cold email sequences, phone calls
- **Tools:** LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Apollo.io, HubSpot CRM
- **Cadence:** 8-touch sequence over 4 weeks
- **Timeline:** Months 2-12 (ongoing)
- **Expected:** 200 outreach, 50 meetings, 20 conversions

**3. Campus Visit & Demo Program ($10,000)**
- **Target:** Regional HBCU clusters (Atlanta, DC, NC Research Triangle, Texas)
- **Approach:** In-person demos, lunch-and-learns, office hours
- **Events:** 2-3 campus visits per month
- **Cost:** Travel, materials, event hosting (~$800 per visit)
- **Expected:** 25 visits, 15 conversions

#### Success Metrics
- **Meetings Booked:** 75 meetings
- **Demo Conversion Rate:** 40%
- **Sales Cycle:** 45 days average
- **CAC:** $1,285 per HBCU customer
- **Revenue:** $170,000 ARR

- Goal: Social proof, inspiration, practical learnings

  - "How Howard University Won $50M in Federal Contracts"
  - "Florida A&M's Journey to R1 Research Status"
  - "From Lab to Contract: Tuskegee's Biotech Success"
